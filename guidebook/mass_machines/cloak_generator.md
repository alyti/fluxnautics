# Was that a bird?

Do you ever feel like there's too many eyes on you? Fret not, for we have just the tool that might be able to help you!

Cloak Generator, as the name implies, generates a cloak around the [Physics Object](/glossary.md#physics-object) its placed on and anything that is connected to the [Physics Object](/glossary.md#physics-object)s.
While the cloak is present anyone that is outside the [Vessel](/glossary.md#vessel)'s bounding box will simply see right through the [Vessel](/glossary.md#vessel).

Only players that are inside the [Vessel](/glossary.md#vessel)'s bounding box will be able to see [Vessel](/glossary.md#vessel). most entities should also become invisible though there are some that don't.
Be sure to test things before assuming they are invisible.

Speaking of bounding boxes, while the cloak is active, if you are outside the [Vessel](/glossary.md#vessel), you also won't see that [Vessel](/glossary.md#vessel)'s bounding box.

## Help, I've lost my ~~keys~~ ship!

Don't panic! It happens to us all one day. First you should get yourself familiar with <ItemImage id="fluxnautics:module_decloaker" />, a module for the MekaSuit helmet.

With this module installed (and active) in the helmet, any cloaks that are produced by generators ***you*** own, will become visible to you once again.
