# What is this "physics" thing you speak of?

## What if you didn't want to deal with "Mass", "Gravity" and "Velocity"?

Sometimes you just don't want to deal with any pesky "realism" in your block game, for such enduring times, enjoy our offering of: Spatial Anchors!

Place it down on a sublevel, give it a lot of power, and no matter what happens the [Physics Object](/glossary.md#physics-object) it was placed on will remain where it was,
the effect is reminiscent of a Physics Staff lock (from Aeronautics).

Unlike said Physics staff lock, velocity that would've affected the sublevel is instead subtracted from the energy buffer of the machine,
if the buffer runs out of power the machine will no longer be able to maintain the spatial lock.
