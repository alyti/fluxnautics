# A big penny for even bigger thrust.

What if I told you there was a way to get more out of each individual thruster?

Well, there is! It's called...

## Boosters!

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="fluxnautics:thruster_array_booster_basic" />
  <ItemIcon id="fluxnautics:thruster_array_booster_advanced" />
  <ItemIcon id="fluxnautics:thruster_array_booster_elite" />
  <ItemIcon id="fluxnautics:thruster_array_booster_ultimate" />
  <ItemIcon id="fluxnautics:thruster_array_booster_creative" />
</ItemGrid>

These are blocks that come in the usual 4 mekanism tiers (basic, advanced, elite, ultimated, plus creative) and they each increase the thruster value cap of the entire thruster array!

For example, in default mod configs: 
1) A thruster array with no boosters has a thrust value cap of 1.0, and a single thruster running at thrust value of 1.0 will output 500pN of thrust in its direction.
    - 10 thrusters would yield 5000pN of thrust
2) A thruster array with a single basic booster has a thrust value cap of 5.0, and a single thruster running at thrust value of 5.0 will output 2500pN of thrust.
    - 10 thrusters would yield 25000pN of thrust

Keep in mind the energy (and possibly hydrogen) usage will also mirror the newly added thrust capacity, be careful how much you use so you don't run out of all your power.
